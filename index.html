Essential Rules for Successful Scholarship Approval

  • ✓
    Aadhaar NPCI Bank Account Seeding: Ensure your bank account is linked to your Aadhaar and NPCI seeding is enabled for direct benefit transfer (DBT).
  • ✓
    Valid Income Certificate: Obtain an Income Certificate issued by Tahsildar / Village Officer valid for the current financial year.
  • ✓
    Marksheet Attestation: Keep your qualifying semester CGPA marksheets / SSLC certificates attested by your Institution Principal / HOD.
  • ✓
    Clear Document Scans: Prepare clear scanned copies (PDF / JPEG under 500KB) of all required certificates prior to starting portal registration.
